{{< team-profile id="_garrett_damore_cto_founder" name="Garrett D’Amore" role="CTO & Founder" portrait="img/garrett-headshot.webp" >}}
Garrett leads engineering at Staysail. He has more than three decades of professional experience building networking, systems, and storage software and leading distributed engineering teams.

He founded [illumos](https://illumos.org/), maintains [nanomsg](https://nanomsg.org/), and has developed software used in [FreeBSD](https://www.freebsd.org/), [NetBSD](https://www.netbsd.org/), [Solaris](https://www.oracle.com/solaris/), and [ZFS](https://openzfs.org/). At Sun Microsystems, he served on the PSARC committee, which guided the technical direction of Solaris.

Garrett has led technical teams at AMD, Qualcomm, Nexenta, Sun Microsystems, and DEY Storage Systems. He holds a bachelor’s degree in computer science from San Diego State University and lives in North San Diego County.
{{< /team-profile >}}

{{< team-profile id="_evgeniia_damore_director_of_finance" name="Evgeniia D’Amore" role="Director of Finance" portrait="img/evgeniia-headshot.webp" >}}
Evgeniia (“Jeny”) oversees day-to-day accounting at Staysail, including customer billing, payroll, and financial reporting.

She previously worked as a senior bank accountant at Snezhinsky Bank in Chelyabinsk, Russia, where she handled consumer mortgage accounts. She holds a master’s degree in finance and economics from the Russian Institute of Economics and lives in North San Diego County.
