**Last updated November 25, 2026**

Staysail Systems, Inc. develops [Jurassic Balloon](/jurassic-balloon/). This policy covers the Android app distributed through Google Play. For privacy questions or deletion requests, email [info@staysail.tech](mailto:info@staysail.tech?subject=Jurassic%20Balloon%20privacy).

## Data the game uses

Jurassic Balloon stores your settings, tutorial history, progress, level unlocks, and achievements in the app’s private storage so you can resume play. The game has no Staysail account, ads, analytics, or in-app telemetry. It does not transmit your save to Staysail or collect your contacts, location, or advertising ID.

## Android backup and Google Play

Depending on your device settings, Android may include the save in its system backup or transfer it when you move to another Android device. Android manages and protects those copies. Staysail cannot access or delete them. You can manage them in your device’s backup settings. [Learn about Android Auto Backup](https://developer.android.com/identity/data/autobackup).

Google handles downloads, updates, and any platform diagnostics under its own policies. Google Play may make aggregated app performance or crash information available to us to help us diagnose problems. [Read Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and anything you choose to attach. We use this information to answer your request and troubleshoot. Our email provider processes the correspondence on our behalf. Access is limited to people who handle support or privacy requests, and we don’t sell support information. If you are a child, ask a parent or guardian to contact us for you.

## Retention and deletion

- To erase your journey, unlocks, achievements, and tutorial history, choose **Options → Reset progress**. This keeps your settings. To remove the entire local save, clear the app’s storage in Android settings. Android may restore a previous copy if you restore from a system backup.
- We keep support correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and related follow-up, or as required by law. You can request deletion by emailing [info@staysail.tech](mailto:info@staysail.tech?subject=Jurassic%20Balloon%20privacy).
