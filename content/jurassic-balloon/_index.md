Your aircraft is wrecked, and dinosaurs are between you and home. You turn your parachute into a hot-air balloon and take to the sky. How hard can it be?

## How to fly

Hold a finger, mouse button, or **Space** to fire the burner and rise. Release to cool and descend. The balloon takes a moment to respond, so leave yourself room to react. On a keyboard, press **Enter** to start or retry and **Escape** to pause or go back.

## The free preview

Fly the five levels of Episode 1, from fern valleys and gathering storms to towering trees and narrow canyons. Choose **Tourist**, **Explorer**, or **Adventurer**. Progress and earned level unlocks are saved by difficulty in the browser you play in. Clearing that browser’s site data can erase your save.

## Where we’re headed

Two episodes are already playable in development builds. We’re aiming for six episodes, with new landscapes, prehistoric encounters, and ways to fly. We’ve built a forward boost for Episode 2 and are exploring ballast, venting, and a Legend difficulty.

We’re building for Android and iOS first, with desktop versions planned. Our goal is to keep Episode 1 free and offer a single purchase to unlock the full game, with ad-free play and DRM-free desktop releases. These plans may change during development.
